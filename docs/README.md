# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory contains the project management guidance used by OctoAcme. These documents describe how cross-functional work moves from an approved idea through planning, execution, delivery, release, and continuous improvement.

## Project management processes at a glance

OctoAcme begins by validating the business need, stakeholders, measurable outcomes, and readiness to proceed. Once approved, the team plans scope, backlog, estimates, milestones, Definition of Done, risks, and dependencies. During execution, work is tracked on a project board, delivered in small reviewed increments, tested, and communicated through regular team and stakeholder cadences. Risks and blockers are monitored and escalated through defined paths. Releases require acceptance criteria, passing CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and a rollback plan. Teams then close milestones with retrospectives, track improvement actions, and measure their impact. Named PM and Product Lead ownership, clear communication, customer value, and iterative delivery underpin the lifecycle.

## Process themes across the docs

- Lifecycle: initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective.
- Ownership: a clear PM and Product Lead drive planning and progress.
- Communication: daily standups, weekly syncs, milestone demos, and stakeholder updates keep work visible and aligned.
- Quality and governance: CI, testing, PR review, acceptance criteria, and security checks reduce delivery risk.
- Improvement: retrospectives and risk tracking convert lessons into concrete action items.

## How to use these docs

Use the overview for a quick orientation, then open the process-specific guide that matches the stage or issue you are handling. Keep the project artifacts updated in the repo so the documentation remains the source of truth for team process and decision-making.
