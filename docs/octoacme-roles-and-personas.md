# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Owners

### Role Summary
Product Owners are responsible for maximizing value by defining priority, scope trade-offs, and acceptance outcomes. They partner closely with project managers and engineering leads to translate strategy into clear delivery decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Prioritize work based on customer and business value
- Clarify requirements, acceptance criteria, and trade-offs
- Make decisions about scope changes and sequencing
- Ensure work aligns with roadmap and stakeholder expectations
- Coordinate with developers and project managers to resolve blockers in delivery

### Goals
- Deliver the right product outcomes at the right time
- Maintain alignment between strategy, backlog, and execution
- Reduce ambiguity and rework through clear prioritization

### Typical Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to confirm business priorities and customer outcomes
- Collaborates with Project Managers on timeline trade-offs, dependencies, and stakeholder communication
- Partners with Developers to clarify acceptance criteria and validate implementation quality

---

## Technical or Delivery Leads

### Role Summary
Technical or Delivery Leads guide the implementation approach, dependency planning, and cross-team execution. They help turn product and project direction into feasible delivery plans while managing technical quality and sequencing.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical planning, architecture decisions, and implementation sequencing
- Identify dependencies, delivery risks, and mitigation options
- Coordinate across engineering functions and shared service teams
- Partner with project managers on staffing, milestones, and progress tracking
- Help resolve technical issues that affect scope, timing, or quality

### Goals
- Keep delivery predictable and technically sound
- Reduce dependency bottlenecks and delivery risk
- Support teams in making effective implementation decisions

### Typical Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to align technical execution, standards, and review expectations
- Supports Product Managers and Product Owners in understanding feasibility and trade-offs
- Coordinates with Project Managers on risk, dependency management, and milestone health

---

## Quality and Testing Leads

### Role Summary
Quality and Testing Leads define how value is validated, what evidence is required, and how defects are managed before release. They ensure the team confirms that outcomes meet quality expectations and user needs.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, release criteria, and quality gates
- Review requirements for testability and coverage needs
- Coordinate testing execution, defect triage, and resolution ownership
- Partner with product and engineering leads on acceptance and readiness decisions
- Help identify gaps in quality, reliability, or operational readiness

### Goals
- Improve release confidence and user trust
- Reduce defects that reach production or customer-facing workflows
- Ensure quality expectations are explicit and measurable

### Typical Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate implementation quality and defect resolution
- Partners with Product Owners and Product Managers to confirm acceptance criteria and readiness
- Collaborates with Project Managers to understand release timing, risk, and communication needs

---

## Change or Communications Leads

### Role Summary
Change or Communications Leads ensure stakeholders understand what is changing, why it matters, and how to adopt it successfully. They connect delivery work with business readiness and organizational alignment.

### Responsibilities
- Develop stakeholder communication plans and rollout messaging
- Coordinate readiness activities, adoption support, and change impacts
- Track stakeholder concerns and provide escalation pathways
- Support stakeholder alignment across business, operations, and delivery teams
- Help ensure transitions are understood and supported before and after launch

### Goals
- Improve stakeholder readiness and minimize disruption
- Increase clarity around priorities, expectations, and change impacts
- Support sustained adoption and value realization

### Typical Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to ensure communications are coordinated with milestones and dependencies
- Partners with Product Owners and Product Managers to align messaging with business priorities
- Supports Developers and Technical Leads by surfacing adoption or operational concerns that affect release readiness
